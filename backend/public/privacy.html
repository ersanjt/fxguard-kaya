Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Kaya Staff iOS and Android apps and the staff portal at kaya.fxguard.io handle information. The apps are a staff portal for authorized employees — not a consumer social app.

Who is responsible

For the hosted product at kaya.fxguard.io, the operator is Ersan Jahed Tabrizi (Kaya CRM). Contact: support@kaya.fxguard.io. If your organization runs its own Kaya CRM server, that organization is the controller of staff and customer data on that server; the mobile app only connects to the URL you enter.

What the mobile apps collect

  • Account credentials you type (email or username, password, optional TOTP) — sent to your organization’s server to sign in. Passwords are not stored on the device.
  • Session token (JWT) stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences (Android) or Keychain (iOS) so you stay signed in.
  • Preferences on the device: language, server URL, and a cached staff profile (name, role) in ordinary app storage.
  • Work content the app loads after login (WhatsApp conversations, customers, tickets, tasks) from your organization’s API. That content belongs to the employer, not to Kaya as a public consumer dataset.

The apps do not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash reporters that sell data, or access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Attachment and voice buttons that are visible but disabled do not read photos or the microphone.

How information is used

Only to operate the staff portal: authenticate you, keep your session, show assigned work, and send messages your role is allowed to send. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Data stays on the device and on the server URL you configure (default https://kaya.fxguard.io). It is not shared with third-party advertisers. Hosting, email, or infrastructure providers used by the server may process data solely to run the service.

Retention and security

The JWT remains until you sign out or uninstall. Server-side retention is set by the organization administrator. Transport uses HTTPS. Tokens are stored in the platform secure store. Production store builds do not allow cleartext HTTP.

Your rights

Staff accounts are created by an administrator, not inside the app. To access, correct, or delete your staff account, ask your admin or email support@kaya.fxguard.io. See also account deletion.

Children

Kaya Staff is a workplace tool. It is not directed at children under 16 (or 13 where that is the applicable threshold) and is not intended for anyone under 18.

International transfers

If you use the default production server, data is processed where that host is located. If you point the app at another server, data is processed under that operator’s location and policies.

Changes

We may update this page. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy for the hosted product.
