Security and procurement summary

This one-pager is for finance and IT reviewers who need an invoice or PO. It is not a SOC 2 / ISO certificate. It describes how the hosted product at kaya.fxguard.io is run today.

  • Cloud Business — from $249 / month — up to 3 branches, 10 staff, FX module. Invoice.

Floors are published so procurement is not guessing. Final commercial numbers can be negotiated. New tiers apply to new signups; existing hosted desks are not silently recapped.

Invoice and PO

Send company name, billing email, tax ID (if any), plan, and seat/branch count via the contact form (purpose: commercial quote / invoice) or WhatsApp. We reply on business days within 24 hours with a written quote. Payment is by invoice for Business and above. Card is only for Cloud Start self-serve. A paid Start checkout does not auto-create a tenant; we provision after the receipt, usually within one business day.

Access and audit

  • Staff accounts only. No public self-signup. Roles from owner through agent. Branch separation.
  • 2FA: TOTP (Google Authenticator) on staff accounts.
  • Sessions: JWT in an httpOnly cookie over HTTPS. Production builds do not allow cleartext HTTP.
  • Audit: activity logs on staff actions. Quoted FX rates can be timestamped in the Business plan.
  • Customer book: chats, tags and notes stay on the company panel — not on a personal phone.

WhatsApp channel — stated honestly

Two paths exist. We will not pretend every deployment is a Meta Business Solution Provider.

  • QR / WhatsApp Web gateway — unofficial session, faster, no Meta Business verification. Bulk/broadcast raises ban risk.
  • Official Meta Cloud API — verified WhatsApp Business API, templates outside the 24-hour window. Required for serious bulk and for buyers who need a compliant channel.

Data location and subprocessors

Hosted data for kaya.fxguard.io is processed on the production host used for that domain. Self-hosted license customers keep data on their own servers. We do not sell personal data. Typical subprocessors (only as needed to run the service):

  • Hosting / DNS / TLS for kaya.fxguard.io
  • Email delivery (transactional and sales inbox)
  • Stripe, only if you pay Cloud Start by card — card data stays with Stripe, not on our servers
  • Meta, only if you connect WhatsApp Cloud API
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, only for the optional staff mobile apps

No advertising SDKs on the staff apps. We do not claim SOC 2 or ISO 27001 on this page. Ask sales if you need a longer questionnaire.

Trial

The public panel is for browsing screens. A live WhatsApp line is a guided session (~10 minutes) or a 7-day own-number trial that the owner starts from the WhatsApp page. The trial does not start just because a QR is scanned. After 7 days the unofficial gateway session is logged out unless the owner keeps the line.
