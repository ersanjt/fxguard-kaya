Delete a staff account

Kaya Staff does not let you create an account in the app. An administrator provisions every user. Apple and Google still require a public way to request deletion.

On this device

Use Sign out in Profile. Uninstalling the app removes the local JWT and preferences from the phone.

On the server

  1. Ask your organization administrator to disable or delete your user in the web panel (Users).
  2. If you cannot reach an admin, email support@kaya.fxguard.io from the address on the account. Include your username and the server URL (for example https://kaya.fxguard.io).

We verify that the request comes from the account holder or an admin before deleting. Customer WhatsApp history belongs to the organization and is not erased solely because one staff user is removed, unless the admin requests that separately and law requires it.
