Questions buyers actually ask

Channel, price, and how to purchase with an invoice.

Is this a generic WhatsApp CRM?

The inbox is included. The product is ownership of the FX customer book: quoted rates, roles, branches and audit. Compare us there — not feature-for-feature with Wati or Chatwoot.

Is WhatsApp official Meta API?

Two paths: unofficial WhatsApp Web (QR) without Business verification — faster, ban risk on bulk — and official Meta Cloud API. We will not hide which one you are on. Cloud API is required for serious broadcast.

Why not $49 unlimited for everyone?

Cloud Start is $49/month for one branch and up to three staff. Business is from $249, Multi-branch from $499. License from $4,000 one-time. Managed from $800/month. Final commercial numbers can be negotiated; the floor is published so procurement is not guessing.

Can we get an invoice / PO instead of WhatsApp checkout?

Yes. Use Request invoice or Get a written quote. Read the security and procurement summary (print to PDF). Cloud Start can be paid by card when Stripe is enabled; otherwise WhatsApp. Commercial and license deals go through invoice.

How do we try it with our own number?

The public panel is for browsing screens. For a live line, ask for a guided session (about 10 minutes) or a time-limited trial where you connect your number and can disconnect it afterwards.
