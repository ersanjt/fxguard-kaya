KVKK data subject application

The Turkish version of this page is the binding text. Translations are provided for convenience.

Under Article 11 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have rights over the personal data we process about you.

Data controller

ERST IT Solutions Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti.

Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

  1. to learn whether your personal data is processed;
  2. to request information about it if it is processed;
  3. to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  4. to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  5. to ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected;
  6. to ask for deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK Article 7;
  7. to ask that corrections and deletions be notified to third parties;
  8. to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis;
  9. to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply

Applications must be in Turkish and made in one of these ways:

  • In writing with a wet signature, to our address above;
  • Through registered electronic mail (KEP);
  • By email signed with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature;
  • By email from an address registered in our systems.

What the application must include

  • your name and surname, and your signature if the application is in writing;
  • your T.R. identity number, or nationality and passport number;
  • your residential or business address for notifications;
  • your email address, phone or fax number, if any;
  • the subject of your request.

Our answer

We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

Complaint to the Board

You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Article 14).
