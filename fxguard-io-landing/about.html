About FXGuard

FXGuard is business software developed and sold by ERST IT Solutions Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti., a software company based in Esenyurt, İstanbul.

What we do

We build a cloud WhatsApp CRM: one company WhatsApp number shared by the team in a web panel, with customer records, roles, branches, two-factor sign-in and an activity log. Customers subscribe monthly (SaaS) or buy a license to run it on their own server. We also provide setup, support and managed hosting.

Who uses it

Small and mid-sized businesses that sell to and support their customers on WhatsApp: exchange offices, clinics, travel and retail teams, agencies and sales desks.

What we do not do

We sell software only. FXGuard and ERST IT Solutions do not buy or sell currency, offer forex or leveraged trading, transfer money, hold customer funds, or provide any payment or investment service. The name “FX” comes from our first customer group, exchange offices, who use the panel to answer their own customers on WhatsApp.

Products and prices

  • Business and Multi-branch: from $249 and $499 per month, quoted in writing.
  • Self-hosted license from $4,000 and Managed hosting from $800 per month, quoted in writing with an invoice.
