Privacy Policy and KVKK notice

This policy has two parts: how we handle the data of customers who buy FXGuard on fxguard.io (KVKK notice), and how the FXGuard Staff iOS and Android apps and the staff panel at app.fxguard.io handle information.

KVKK notice for customers and buyers

This section explains how we process the personal data of people who buy FXGuard or ask for a quote on fxguard.io, under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

  • Data controller: ERST IT Solutions Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti., Esenyurt / İstanbul. Company details are on the contact page.
  • Data we process: name or company name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, billing address and tax details, order and payment records, and support messages. Card details are processed by the payment institution iyzico; they are not sent to us and we do not store them.
  • Purposes: making and performing the contract, opening the panel account, invoicing, payments and refunds, support, legal obligations, and service security.
  • Legal grounds: KVKK Article 5(2): (c) making and performing a contract, (ç) legal obligation (tax and commercial law), (e) establishing, exercising or protecting a right, and (f) legitimate interest.
  • Transfers: to the payment institution (iyzico), e-invoice and accounting providers, hosting and email providers, and authorized public bodies on request, only as far as these purposes require. For international orders, Paddle collects payment and billing details directly as reseller. Any transfer abroad is made in line with KVKK Article 9.
  • Cookies: fxguard.io uses no advertising or analytics cookies. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Retention: invoices and accounting records are kept for the periods set by tax and commercial law (usually 10 years). Other data is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.
  • Panel data: for the customer and message data a client stores in its FXGuard panel, the client is the data controller; we process it only as a processor, to provide the service.
  • Your rights (KVKK Article 11): to learn whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, object, and claim compensation. How to apply, and a printable form, are on the KVKK application page; we answer within 30 days.

FXGuard Staff apps and staff panel

Who is responsible

For the hosted product at app.fxguard.io, the seller is ERST IT Solutions Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. (product: FXGuard). Contact: support@fxguard.io. If your organization runs its own FXGuard server, that organization is the controller of staff and customer data on that server.

What the mobile apps collect

  • Account credentials you type (email or username, password, optional TOTP) — sent to your organization’s server to sign in. Passwords are not stored on the device.
  • Session token (JWT) stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences (Android) or Keychain (iOS) so you stay signed in.
  • Preferences on the device: language, server URL, and a cached staff profile.
  • Work content the app loads after login (WhatsApp conversations, customers, tickets, tasks) from your organization’s API.

The apps do not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers that sell data, or access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

How information is used

Only to operate the staff portal: authenticate you, keep your session, show assigned work, and send messages your role allows. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Data stays on the device and on the server URL you configure (default https://app.fxguard.io). It is not shared with third-party advertisers.

Retention and security

The JWT remains until you sign out or uninstall. Server-side retention is set by the organization administrator. Transport uses HTTPS. Tokens are stored in the platform secure store.

Your rights

Staff accounts are created by an administrator. To access, correct, or delete your staff account, ask your admin or email support@fxguard.io. See also account deletion.

Children

FXGuard Staff is a workplace tool. It is not directed at children under 16 and is not intended for anyone under 18.

International transfers

If you use the default production server, data is processed where that host is located. Self-hosted license customers keep data on their own infrastructure.
