How WhatsApp is connected — two honest paths

We will not pretend every deployment is a Meta Business Solution Provider. Choose the path that matches your market.

QR gateway (WhatsApp Web)

Scan once, staff reply from the panel. Fast, no Meta Business verification. Unofficial session: bulk/broadcast raises ban risk. Use it when official API is not available.

Official Meta Cloud API

Verified WhatsApp Business API. No QR. Template messages outside the 24-hour window. Required for serious bulk and for buyers who need a compliant channel.

Questions buyers actually ask

Channel, price floors, and how to purchase with an invoice.

Why not respond.io, Wati, Kommo, Trengo or Chatwoot?

Those are conversation inboxes for ads and support — Instagram, TikTok, AI Agents, HubSpot. FXGuard is an operations panel for exchange, remittance and finance desks: FX rates on the same screen as WhatsApp, a company-owned customer book, branches, 2FA, and a license if data cannot sit on a US SaaS. Inbox is included. If you need ads plus Instagram, pick them.

Is WhatsApp official Meta API?

Two paths: unofficial WhatsApp Web (QR) without Business verification — faster, bulk raises ban risk — and official Meta Cloud API. We do not hide which one you are on. Serious broadcast needs Cloud API.

Why not $49 unlimited for everyone?

Cloud Start is $49/month for one branch and up to three staff, without the FX module. Business from $249. Multi-branch from $499. License from $4,000 one-time. Managed from $800/month. Final numbers can be negotiated; the floor is published.

Can we get an invoice / PO instead of WhatsApp checkout?

Yes. Use Request invoice or Get a written quote. Read the security and procurement summary (print to PDF). Cloud Start is WhatsApp. Commercial and license deals go through invoice.

How do we try it with our own number?

We do not publish a shared demo password here. Book a 10-minute guided session, or a time-limited trial where you connect your number and can disconnect it afterwards.

Can FXGuard handle multiple branches?

Yes. Branches and departments are built in, with owner-level oversight. Multi-branch cloud starts from $499/month for new signups.

What languages do you support?

This website supports English, Persian, Turkish, Arabic and Russian. The panel is customized per business — sales teams, shops, clinics, companies, and FX desks that need the rates module.

How do I get started?

Book a guided demo, then Cloud Start on WhatsApp or a written quote for Business, license or managed. We guide onboarding after that.

If I am not satisfied, do I get my money back?

Yes for the first Cloud Start month: message WhatsApp within 7 days of the first payment, disconnect the number, and we refund that month. Business, multi-branch, license and managed refunds follow the invoice. We do not publish a fake unlimited guarantee.

Does AI run the desk instead of staff?

No. Optional OpenAI first-replies and routing — you bring the key. AI does not quote a live rate, does not process files, and does not replace roles or 2FA. Staff close the deal.
