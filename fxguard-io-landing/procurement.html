Access and audit

  • Staff accounts only. No public self-signup. Roles from owner through agent. Branch separation.
  • 2FA: TOTP (Google Authenticator) on staff accounts.
  • Sessions: JWT in an httpOnly cookie over HTTPS.
  • Audit: activity logs on staff actions. Quoted FX rates can be timestamped on Business and above.
  • Customer book: chats, tags and notes stay on the company panel — not on a personal phone.

WhatsApp channel — stated honestly

Two paths exist. We will not pretend every deployment is a Meta Business Solution Provider.

  • QR / WhatsApp Web gateway — unofficial session, faster, no Meta Business verification. Bulk/broadcast raises ban risk.
  • Official Meta Cloud API — verified WhatsApp Business API, templates outside the 24-hour window. Required for serious bulk and for buyers who need a compliant channel.

Data location and subprocessors

Hosted data for app.fxguard.io is processed on the production host used for that domain. Self-hosted license customers keep data on their own servers. We do not sell personal data. Typical subprocessors (only as needed to run the service):

  • Hosting / DNS / TLS for fxguard.io and app.fxguard.io
  • Email delivery (transactional and sales inbox)
  • Meta, only if you connect WhatsApp Cloud API
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, only for the optional staff mobile apps

No advertising SDKs on the staff apps. We do not claim SOC 2 or ISO 27001 on this page. EU buyers: DPA on request — see also fxguard.io/eu. Ask sales if you need a longer questionnaire.

Trial
