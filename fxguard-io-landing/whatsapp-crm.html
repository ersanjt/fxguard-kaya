Built for exchange, remittance and finance desks

FXGuard is not a generic ads inbox. The usual buyer is a money desk: one company WhatsApp number, quoted FX rates on the same panel as the chat, a customer book that stays when staff leave, and roles per branch. Other teams can run Cloud Start without the FX pack.

Two honest WhatsApp paths

Unofficial QR (WhatsApp Web session) is faster to start and is not Meta-certified. Official Cloud API is the path when you need a documented business line. We say which one you would actually run — we do not sell “official WhatsApp” on a QR session.

Cloud Start $49 vs Business from $249

Start is 1 branch, 3 staff, inbox/tickets/tasks — no FX rates module. Business adds FX, more seats and branches. License and Managed are quoted from published floors. Accounting and exchange websites are separate products.
