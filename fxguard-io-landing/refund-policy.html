Delivery and Refund Policy

The Turkish version of this policy is the binding text. Translations are provided for convenience.

FXGuard is a digital software service. This page explains how the service is delivered, how to cancel, and when we refund.

Delivery

  • There is no physical product or shipping; the service is provided over the internet.
  • Panel access is opened within 1 business day after the payment is confirmed. Sign-in details are sent to the email or WhatsApp number you gave at checkout.
  • Connecting your own WhatsApp number can take longer, depending on Meta’s approval process; we help you through that step.

Cancellation

Payments through iyzico do not renew automatically. Subscriptions bought through Paddle can be cancelled at any time by WhatsApp or email; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

  • First month of Cloud Start: if you ask within 7 days of the first payment and disconnect your WhatsApp number from the panel, we refund that month in full.
  • Service not delivered: if panel access is not opened within 1 business day, or the service cannot be used for a reason on our side, we refund the amount paid.
  • Wrong or duplicate charge: refunded in full.
  • Business, Multi-branch, license and managed hosting: the refund terms in the written quote and invoice apply.

Outside these cases, a subscription period that has already started is not partly refunded.

How to ask for a refund

Write to support@fxguard.io or WhatsApp +90 501 067 6486 with your order email and payment date. We answer within 3 business days at the latest.

Refund timing

An approved refund is sent to the card you paid with, through iyzico, within 14 days at the latest. For international orders paid through Paddle, Paddle sends the refund to the original payment method. How long it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

Consumers’ statutory rights are not affected. See the Distance Sales Agreement for details.
