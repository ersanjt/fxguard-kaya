Staff account deletion

FXGuard Staff does not create accounts inside the app. Your administrator creates each user. Apple and Google still require a public deletion path.

On this device

Use Sign out in profile. Uninstalling removes the local JWT and preferences.

On the server

  1. Ask your organization administrator to deactivate or delete your user in the web panel (Users).
  2. If you cannot reach an admin, email support@fxguard.io from your staff account email with username and server URL.

We verify identity before deletion. Customer WhatsApp history belongs to the organization and is not erased when one staff user is removed unless the admin requests it and law requires.
