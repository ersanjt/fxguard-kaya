Cookie Policy

The Turkish version of this policy is the binding text. Translations are provided for convenience.

This page explains what fxguard.io and the FXGuard panel at app.fxguard.io store in your browser (cookies, local storage and session storage), why, and how you can delete it.

No advertising or analytics cookies

We do not use advertising, analytics, profiling or social-media tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party trackers.

What fxguard.io stores

Name Type Purpose Lifetime
landing_lang Local storage Remembers the language you chose on the site. Until you clear it
fxg_legal_lang Local storage Remembers the language you chose on legal pages. Until you clear it
fxg_utm Session storage Only if you arrived through a campaign link: the campaign name. Deleted when you close the tab
crm_token (app.fxguard.io) Cookie (HttpOnly) Keeps you signed in to the panel. 7 days, or until you sign out

What the app.fxguard.io panel stores

After you sign in, the panel uses the session cookie above and local storage for interface preferences you set yourself.

Legal basis

All of the items above are strictly necessary for a service you asked for.

Third-party sites

WhatsApp (wa.me) links and the iyzico or Paddle payment pages open only when you click them.

How to delete or block

You can delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings.
