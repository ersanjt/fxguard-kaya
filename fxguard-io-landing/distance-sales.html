Pre-contract Information and Distance Sales Agreement

The Turkish version of this agreement is the binding text. Translations are provided for convenience.

This text covers the FXGuard software service bought online at fxguard.io. If the buyer is a consumer, it is also the pre-contract information required by Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation. By completing payment, the buyer confirms having read and accepted it electronically.

1. Parties

Seller: ERST IT Solutions Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. (“Seller”), details below.

Buyer: the person or company whose name, email, phone and billing details are entered at checkout (“Buyer”).

2. Subject

The rights and obligations of the parties for the sale and delivery of the FXGuard software subscription or license that the Buyer orders at fxguard.io.

3. Service and price

FXGuard is cloud WhatsApp customer-management software for businesses (SaaS). The plan, its scope (branches and users) and the billing period are shown on the payment page.

The total amount including taxes, and its currency, are shown on the payment page before the Buyer confirms. There is no shipping or delivery fee.

Only software is sold. The Seller does not provide currency exchange, forex, money transfer, payment or investment services.

4. Payment

Payment is taken by credit or debit card through iyzico (İyzi Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Hizmetleri A.Ş.), a licensed payment institution. The Seller never sees or stores card details.

A payment through iyzico covers one period and does not renew automatically. Subscriptions bought through Paddle renew automatically until cancelled.

International orders: Buyers outside Turkey may pay through Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), which acts as reseller and Merchant of Record for those orders.

5. Delivery

The service is delivered online; nothing is shipped. Panel access is opened within 1 (one) business day after the payment is confirmed, and sign-in details are sent to the Buyer’s email or WhatsApp number. If access is not opened within that time, the Buyer may ask for a refund of the amount paid.

6. Right of withdrawal

Under Article 15 of the Distance Contracts Regulation, the right of withdrawal does not apply to services performed instantly online, or to services whose performance starts with the Buyer’s approval before the withdrawal period ends. At checkout the Buyer asks for the service to start immediately and accepts that the right of withdrawal ends for that reason.

Even so, the Seller gives a 7-day money-back guarantee on the first month of Cloud Start, counted from the first payment (see section 7).

If the Buyer is a business buying for commercial purposes, consumer law does not apply; this agreement and the written quote govern.

7. Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund terms are on the Delivery and Refund Policy page, which forms part of this agreement. Approved refunds are sent to the card used for payment through iyzico within 14 days at the latest; how long the bank takes to show it depends on the bank.

8. Buyer obligations

The Buyer agrees to give accurate details at checkout, not to share account credentials with unauthorized people, to follow WhatsApp/Meta policies and applicable law, and not to use the service for unlawful purposes. The Buyer is the data controller for the customer data it stores in the panel; the Seller processes that data only as a processor, to provide the service.

9. Personal data

The Buyer’s personal data is processed under the Privacy Policy and KVKK notice.

10. Disputes

A consumer Buyer may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee or Consumer Court at their place of residence or where the transaction was made, within the monetary limits announced each year by the Turkish Ministry of Trade. Disputes between businesses are subject to the courts and enforcement offices of İstanbul.

11. Effect

The Buyer accepts all terms of this agreement when confirming the payment. The Seller keeps the agreement electronically and sends a copy to the Buyer’s email on request.
